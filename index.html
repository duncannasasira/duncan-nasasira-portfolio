About Me

My name is Duncan Nasasira, an educator with a background in Mathematics and Computer Education and a strong passion for teaching, technology, innovation, and lifelong learning.

I currently teach Mathematics and Information and Communication Technology (ICT), helping learners develop analytical thinking, problem-solving abilities, digital literacy, and practical skills they can apply in everyday life.

In addition to my teaching responsibilities, I serve as a Computer Laboratory Attendant at Kabale Secondary School, where I support the effective use, organisation, and maintenance of computer laboratory resources.

This experience has strengthened my interest in computer systems, laboratory management, information technology, and developing digital solutions that improve the delivery of education.
